# statgenGWAS: Genome Wide Association Studies

Fast single trait Genome Wide Association Studies (GWAS) following the
method described in Kang et al. (2010),
[doi:10.1038/ng.548](https://doi.org/10.1038/ng.548) . One of a series
of statistical genetic packages for streamlining the analysis of typical
plant breeding experiments developed by Biometris.
